The Author with a Purpose

Trinity Renee Gibbs, who goes by Renee, is fourteen years old, and poetry is how she makes sense of a life that hasn't always been easy. When she isn't writing, she's usually reading thrillers and mysteries, or listening to R&B, hip hop, rap, soul, and blues.

The Weight of the Word

Words are how I take what tried to break me and turn it into something that builds. These four trilogies trace that turn: claiming space after being underestimated, meeting betrayal with composure and sharp humor instead of bitterness, rebuilding the child I used to be into someone steady, and finally speaking as a witness who refuses to stay silent. Every poem here is proof that a voice can carry real weight and still rise.

The Road Ahead

One day I want to publish a full poetry collection. This page is the very beginning of that dream, and every person who reads these words is part of the reason I keep going. Thank you for being here.

“The Lord is close to the brokenhearted and saves those who are crushed in spirit.”
— Psalm 34:18

You’ve just experienced the realest real,
Your trauma made you stronger? It absolutely did not, it made me funnier though.

  • “I survived the emotional damage of my youth, and all I got was this lousy coping mechanism.”
  • “I asked my therapist if she could fix my inner child. She said at least the trauma made me funny.”
